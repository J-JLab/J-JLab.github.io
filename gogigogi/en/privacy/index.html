Gogigogi Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

J.JLab ("we") makes the iOS app Gogigogi ("the app") and handles personal information under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and related laws. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. If this translation and the Korean policy differ, the Korean policy prevails.

1. Information we collect

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or anything else that identifies you, and the app sends nothing to our servers.

2. Information kept only on your device

  • Your chosen language, the animal you looked at last, and whether you have seen the first-use tip
  • Your best quiz score

The app keeps these on your device and never sends them anywhere.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows an ad from Google LLC's AdMob at the bottom of the screen. All ads are limited to the General (G) rating. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you choose "Allow" when the app asks to track
  • IP address, device and operating system information, approximate location (from the IP address)
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps, and the ad SDK's diagnostics and performance data

If you allow tracking, Google may show ads based on your interests (personalized ads).
If you do not, the app asks for non-personalized ads on every request and turns off the app-scoped ad identifier (publisher first-party ID). The IP address, device information and ad interactions above are still processed to serve and measure ads.

You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's handling is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address may reach Google. We do not receive it.

To refuse, stop using or delete the app. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not sent, but the IP address, device information and ad interactions are still sent while you use the app.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep no personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • Information on your device (section 2) is removed by iOS when you delete the app.
  • Information Google processes is kept and deleted as Google's privacy policy describes.

7. Security

  • We do not keep personal information on any server.
  • What the app stores stays in its iOS sandbox, out of reach of other apps.
  • Ad and font traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Ads are limited to the General (G) rating.

9. Your rights

  • What we hold: nothing that identifies you, so there is nothing to show, correct or delete. Contact us (section 10) with any question.
  • On your device: delete the app.
  • What Google processes: stop sharing the advertising identifier with the tracking setting in section 3, and ask Google to stop processing or delete data through the Google privacy form.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with its effective date.
