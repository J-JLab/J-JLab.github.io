Gogigogi Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

J.JLab ("we") makes the iOS app Gogigogi ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Information we collect

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or anything else that identifies you, and the app sends nothing to our servers.

2. Information kept only on your device

  • Your chosen language and your best quiz score

The app keeps these on your device and never sends them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows an ad from Google LLC's AdMob at the bottom of the screen. To serve and measure ads, Google may process:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you choose "Allow" when the app asks to track
  • IP address, device and operating system information, approximate location (from the IP address)
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps

If you do not allow tracking, you see ads that are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's handling is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address may reach Google. We do not receive it.

5. Transfers outside Korea

For sections 3 and 4, information goes to Google LLC in the United States and other countries where Google operates: the advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address, device and OS information and ad interactions, sent over the network while you use the app, to serve and measure ads and deliver fonts, and kept as Google's privacy policy describes. If you do not want this, stop using or delete the app; if you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not sent.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Your choices

We hold no information that identifies you, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. Delete the app to remove what it stored on your device, and manage ad tracking as described in section 3.

8. Contact

Privacy officer: the J.JLab operator
Contact: GitHub issues

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with its effective date.
