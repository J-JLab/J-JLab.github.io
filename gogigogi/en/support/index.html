Gogigogi Support

Gogigogi is a guide to pork, beef and fish cuts: explore each animal as a 3D model and learn what every cut looks like and how it is used. It speaks Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French and Italian.

Questions

How do I change the language?
Tap the globe at the top of the screen. At first the app follows your device language.

How do I clear my quiz score?
It lives only on your device. Delete and reinstall the app to clear it.

How do I turn off personalized ads?
In Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, turn Gogigogi off. See the privacy policy for details.

Contact

Report a problem or suggest something on GitHub issues. Include your device model, iOS version and app version so we can look quickly. Issues are public, so please leave out personal information.
